RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made on this 14th day of September, 2026, between:

LANDLORD: Mr. Rajesh Sharma, residing at 123 Green Park, New Delhi, India.
TENANT: Mr. Aayush Kumar Singh, residing at 456 Sky Tower, Mumbai, India.

1. PROPERTY: The Landlord agrees to rent the property located at Flat 402, Azure Apartments, Sector 62, Noida, UP.

2. TERM: The term of this agreement is for 11 months, starting from October 1, 2026.

3. RENT: The monthly rent for the property is ₹25,000, payable on the 5th of every month. A late fee of ₹500 per day shall be applied for payments after the 10th.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT: The Tenant shall pay a security deposit of ₹1,00,000. This deposit is non-refundable if the tenant leaves before the term ends.

5. TERMINATION: 
   a. The Tenant may terminate this agreement by giving 60 days' written notice.
   b. The Landlord may terminate this agreement for any reason by giving 15 days' written notice.

6. AUTOMATIC RENEWAL: This agreement shall automatically renew for another 11 months unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least 30 days prior to the expiry date.

7. MAINTENANCE: The Tenant is responsible for all minor repairs below ₹2,000. Major structural repairs shall be borne by the Landlord.

8. GOVERNING LAW: This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Uttar Pradesh and the courts of Noida shall have jurisdiction.
