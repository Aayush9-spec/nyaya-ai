Consumer Protection Act, 2019
Section 2(7): "Consumer" means any person who buys any goods for a consideration... but does not include a person who obtains such goods for resale.
Section 34: Provisions relating to the jurisdiction of District Commissions.
Section 35: Manner in which complaint shall be made.
The act allows consumers to file complaints for defective goods or deficient services, including the right to refund, replacement, or compensation.
