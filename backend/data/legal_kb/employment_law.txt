Industrial Disputes Act, 1947
Section 2(s): "Worker" means any person employed in any industry to do any manual, unskilled, skilled, technical, operational, clerical or supervisory work for hire or reward.
Section 25F: Conditions precedent to retrenchment of workmen. Requires one month's notice in writing indicating the reasons for retrenchment and payment of compensation.
Payment of Wages Act, 1936
Section 5: Wages must be paid within a specified time limit (usually 7th or 10th of the month).
